prune gradle cache:
 The cleanest approach: stop daemons, then wipe the entire cache. Gradle re-downloads only what the next build actually needs (much less than 3.2 GB).

  cd ~/Projects/org.stephe_leake.music_player
  ./gradlew --stop
  rm -rf ~/.gradle/caches/

  If you'd rather keep the downloaded artifacts (avoid re-downloading) and only free the regenerable working cache:

  ./gradlew --stop
  rm -rf ~/.gradle/caches/9.0-milestone-1/
  rm -rf ~/.gradle/caches/jars-9/
